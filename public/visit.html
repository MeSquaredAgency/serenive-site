FAQs

Good to know before you visit

  • Do I need to book?

    Yes. To ensure we provide the utmost in care and indulgence, we prefer you to book in advance. Bookings can be made online or by calling us on (08) 8182 4682.

  • What should I bring to my appointment?

    Just yourself. We’ll provide robes, towels and everything else to make your visit comfortable and relaxing.

  • Are gift cards available?

    They certainly are! Give the gift of choice and send the perfect present with an e-gift card, bought online, or a wrapped gift card from the spa. See our gift cards.

  • Do you use natural and organic products?

    Absolutely! At Serenive, we believe what you put on your skin matters. That’s why we use only natural and organic products, free from harmful chemicals, gentle on your body and kind to the environment. It’s a healthier choice for your skin and a more sustainable choice for our planet. Because beauty should never come at a cost to your wellbeing, or the earth’s.

  • Are the products used in my treatment available to purchase?

    Yes! We’ve carefully selected natural and organic products for our treatments because they’re gentle, effective and better for your skin and the planet. Now you can continue that same level of care at home with our spa-quality products, available to purchase, so your self-care doesn’t have to end when your treatment does.
